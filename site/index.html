Play safe

  • Crash multipliers are random. A run of low rounds never means a high one is “due”.
  • Every fixed cash-out target has the same expected return, so none beats the house edge.
  • Decide your limit before you start, and stop when you reach it.
  • Never chase losses or borrow money to gamble.
  • If gambling affects your money, work, relationships or wellbeing, take a break and contact a local support service or someone you trust.
